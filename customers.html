The daily loop

From stand-up to done, in one place

The loop a team like Cognecto's runs on TeamUp. Every step below is a feature in TeamUp 1.0.

  1. MorningThe stand-up opens for the teams it is for. Attendance comes from the call, with leave and holidays shown.
  2. Right afterThe scrum table: each person, their lead, Task 1, Task 2 and on, what they finished, and what is blocked and why.
  3. All dayChannels, threads and @mentions, with a call one tap away from any conversation.
  4. After each callMinutes with decisions and owners go to everybody who was there.
  5. On the calendarCalls show for the time each person was really in them.

01

Engineering and product teams

Stand-up, scrum, build, review. The loop engineers run every day, without four tools and four logins.

Scrum tableBoardDue datesThreadsScreen shareMeeting notes
  1. A stand-up for Engineering onlyA standing meeting for chosen teams, on the days and time you set, with attendance from the call itself.
  2. The scrum as a tableEach person, their lead, Task 1, Task 2 and on, Done, and Blocked on with the reason. Each viewer arranges people in their own order. Try it
  3. Talk it through, then callThreads and @mentions keep the discussion together, and screen share works without the endless mirror.
  4. Minutes become action itemsSummary, decisions and action items with owners, sent to everybody who was in the call.

02

Field operations

People on the move, on phones, on patchy networks. TeamUp keeps them talking.

Installs on phonesVoice messagesWeak-connection modeAudio onlyLaptop and phone
  1. On the home screenTeamUp installs to an Android or iPhone home screen and opens full screen. There is also an Android app that opens your server.
  2. Report with voice and videoVoice and video messages recorded on the spot in any conversation, with several files at once.
  3. Calls that hold on weak networksVoice comes first: the camera pauses with a reason everyone sees, and Audio only saves data. Try it
  4. Laptop and phone in one callJoin from both for a second angle. Everybody still counts you once.

03

HR and attendance

Know who was there, who is on leave and which days are holidays, without chasing a spreadsheet.

AttendanceLeaveHolidaysCustom rolesTeam treeWelcome email
  1. Attendance from the callStand-ups record who joined and when, from the call server's own events, not from a form.
  2. Leave and holidaysLeave and organization holidays show in stand-up attendance. The person, their manager or an administrator records leave.
  3. Onboarding in one stepEvery new member gets a welcome email with sign-in details, then a short welcome tour at first sign-in.
  4. Roles and teamsCustom roles with fine-grained permissions, a team tree, and managers who can read the calendars of the people on their teams.

04

Agencies and consultancies

Client calls that look like your company, and notes nobody has to write.

Guests by linkCompany backgroundsYour colour and logoMeeting notesPrivate channels
  1. Clients join by linkGuests join a meeting with their name and a passcode, and the organizer can remove them.
  2. Look like your companyCompany call backgrounds carry each person's name, and the app shows your organization's colour and logo.
  3. Minutes after every client callSummary, decisions and action items with owners, kept under Meeting notes.
  4. Keep clients apartPrivate channels for each client, and search that only shows what each person may see.

05

Schools and training

Classes and cohorts that meet on a schedule, with a record of who came.

Standing meetingsAttendanceRaise handScreen shareAnnouncementsGuests
  1. A standing class for each cohortStanding meetings for chosen teams or people, on the days and time you set.
  2. Attendance every sessionTaken from the call itself, with leave and holidays marked.
  3. Hands up, screens sharedRaised hands queue in order, and the teacher shares a screen to the whole class.
  4. Announcements and guest speakersAnnouncement channels for the whole school, and guest speakers who join by link with a passcode.
